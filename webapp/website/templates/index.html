About Us

Welcome to Surya Hospital, a trusted name in healthcare dedicated to delivering compassionate, advanced, and affordable medical services for everyone. Since our inception, we have been committed to providing comprehensive healthcare under one roof — blending medical excellence with a patient-centered approach. At Surya Hospital, we believe that good health is the foundation of a happy life. Our team of experienced doctors, skilled nurses, and caring staff work round the clock to ensure every patient receives personalized attention and the highest quality of care. Equipped with modern technology and state-of-the-art facilities, we aim to make accurate diagnosis and effective treatment accessible to all. From emergency care and specialized treatments to preventive health checkups, Surya Hospital stands as a beacon of hope and healing in the community. Our mission is not just to treat illness but to promote wellness and empower individuals to lead healthier lives. At Surya Hospital, your health and comfort are our top priorities — because we care, every step of the way.


At Surya Hospital, we take pride in offering the most advanced medical technology and world-class facilities to ensure every patient receives safe, accurate, and efficient care. Our hospital is equipped with modern infrastructure and state-of-the-art equipment that meet international standards, enabling our medical team to provide the highest level of diagnosis and treatment. Our operation theatres are designed with advanced surgical instruments and strict infection control systems, ensuring precision and safety during every procedure. The hospital also features a 24×7 emergency and trauma care unit, fully equipped with modern life-support systems and continuous monitoring facilities. To aid in accurate diagnosis, we have a high-definition radiology and imaging center with digital X-ray, ultrasound, and CT scan services, along with a fully automated pathology laboratory that delivers fast and reliable results. For critical patients, our ICU and NICU units are equipped with advanced ventilators and real-time monitoring systems, providing intensive care around the clock. We also offer 24-hour pharmacy and ambulance services to ensure timely support during emergencies. Every patient room and ward at Surya Hospital is designed with comfort and hygiene in mind, creating a calm and healing environment. Through our modern equipment, advanced technology, and compassionate service, Surya Hospital strives to deliver excellence in healthcare and set new standards in patient well-being.
